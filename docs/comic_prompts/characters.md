# Character Definitions - The Sand Pit: Sand Crab vs Sea Anemone

**Style**: ligne-claire (清线)
**Art Direction**: Clean, uniform outlines, flat colors, European comic tradition with marine science-illustration warmth. Think Tintin meets underwater nature documentary.

---

## Character 1: Agent A — The Sand Crab (沙蟹)

**Role**: Protagonist / Pursuer
**Age**: Young adult (energetic but not fully mature)

**Appearance**:
- Face shape: Round, slightly flattened with a permanent eager expression
- Eyes: Large, round, expressive amber eyes on stalks that tilt with emotion
- Build: Compact body, about the size of a tennis ball, sturdy carapace
- Distinguishing features: One slightly oversized left claw (asymmetric, adds goofiness), small sand particles stuck to legs

**Costume**:
- Default outfit: Smooth sand-colored carapace (warm beige #D4A373) with faint darker speckles
- Color palette: Warm sand (#D4A373), coral red claw tips (#E76F51), amber eyes (#F4A261)
- Accessories: None (natural look)

**Expression Range**:
- Neutral: Scuttling forward with determination, eyes focused ahead
- Happy/Excited: Eyes wide and bright, claws raised in anticipation, slight bounce in movement
- Thinking/Confused: One eye stalk tilted higher than the other, thought bubbles appearing, claws frozen mid-air
- Determined: Both claws forward, body low to ground, eyes narrowed with intense focus
- Defeated/Nervous: Claws drooping, one eye stalk sagging, small sand cloud around feet

**Movement Style**:
- Sideways scuttle with quick, choppy leg movements
- Kicks up small puffs of sand when moving fast
- Stops abruptly by digging claws into sand

## Character 2: Agent B — The Sea Anemone (海葵)

**Role**: Antagonist / Target (immobile, but psychologically dominant)
**Age**: Mature (wise, calm, unshakable)

**Appearance**:
- Base: Soft, bulbous column body rooted firmly in pale pink coral rock
- Tentacles: 12-16 flowing tentacles in gradient pink (#F4A3C0 to #E85D8A), translucent tips
- Eyes: No traditional eyes — expression conveyed entirely through tentacle posture and slight "facial" indentation at center
- Build: Medium size, about twice the crab's height when tentacles fully extended
- Distinguishing features: Center mouth area forms a subtle smirk-like curve; tentacles move with independent, fluid grace

**Costume**:
- Default outfit: Natural anemone form — no clothing needed
- Color palette: Soft pink (#F4A3C0), magenta (#E85D8A), cream base (#FFF8F0), pale teal rock (#A8DADC)
- Accessories: Tiny bioluminescent spots at tentacle bases (glow faintly in darker panels)

**Expression Range**:
- Neutral/Calm: Tentacles swaying gently in water current, perfectly symmetrical
- Smug/Mocking: Center tentacles raised slightly higher, outer tentacles relaxed, subtle "eyebrow" tilt
- Nervous: Tentacles retracting inward, tips curling, bioluminescent spots dimming
- Surprised: All tentacles flaring outward, center mouth gaping slightly
- Defeated: Tentacles limp and drooping, losing their usual graceful curve

**Movement Style**:
- Immobile base, but tentacles wave with hypnotic, independent motion
- Reacts to nearby threats with rapid tentacle retraction
